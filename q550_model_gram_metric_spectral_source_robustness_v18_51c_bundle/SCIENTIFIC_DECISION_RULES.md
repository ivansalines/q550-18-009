# Scientific decision rules — v18.51c

1. **Authenticity:** Verify CRC and SHA-256 of the v18.51b sendback and embedded v18.51a, v18.50z, v18.50y, and v18.50x parents. Abort on any mismatch.
2. **Base-case regression:** The Euclidean channel coupling rate, first singular strength, and canonical fraction must agree with v18.51b to absolute error below 3e-6. This is not an independent discovery; it guards against changing the observable.
3. **Metric family:** Use fixed alpha=0,0.1,1,4 and evolving alpha=1 with a *common across seeds* symmetrized Jacobian-Gram metric. Demand positive definiteness; record condition numbers.
4. **Two different comparisons:** (i) A physical metric change can alter the reported angular/spectral results; (ii) a passive non-orthogonal coordinate transformation of the same metric and operators must leave them unchanged after orthogonal identification. The second is the covariance control, not evidence of metric independence.
5. **Source accounting:** Split generator derivative, projector-posture derivative, canonical observer derivative, and—only when the metric changes—the metric-whitening derivative. Signed Frobenius shares must sum to 1 within 1e-10. Record negative shares, do not truncate them.
6. **Spectrum:** Track all non-negligible pairs generated by weighted metric-skew reduction. Do not assume the Euclidean near-null third pair stays zero under metric changes. Require adequate spectral gaps for the leading 2+2 block.
7. **Derivative quality:** Compare directly spline-differentiated weighted defect with the fully source-decomposed derivative. Threshold 2e-4 (empirical diagnostic, not certified tolerance).
8. **Interpretation:** Call an attribution *robust across the tested metric family* only if its sign/dominance and direction of evolution remain stable. Do not call it invariant under all metrics, physically kinetic, material memory, or causal energy transfer.
9. **Reproducibility:** Source manifest immutable. Output manifest rewritten from regenerated results. Exclude temporary directories, bytecode and logs; match external ZIP name and inner root.
