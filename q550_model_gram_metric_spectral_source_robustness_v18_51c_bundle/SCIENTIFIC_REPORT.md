# Q550 v18.51c — Scientific report

## Question

Does the spectral-channel attribution from v18.51b remain recognizable when the Euclidean metric is replaced by a model-derived, regularized, positive-definite Jacobian-Gram family?

## Dataset and construction

The comparison reuses 79 sections per seed, two independently prepared natural seeds, and the **same midpoint kappa** in both. Results are post-processing of authenticated v18.51b/z/y/x tensors, with **no new Q550 IVPs**. A common model-Gram reference is assembled from the two `J_10` matrices. This is a tangent-Jacobian-informed observer metric, **not** a derived Q550 kinetic-energy metric.

Weighted generators are analyzed after whitening and metric-skew reduction. The original source terms are transformed linearly; in the evolving metric an explicit additional metric-rate term is included so that the sum reconstructs the weighted derivative.

Signed contributions add to 100%, including the negative metric-rate term. The autonomous preparation shows nearly identical patterns, without identical numbers. The main result is **tested-family persistence** of the canonical-source dominance and of its rising share, not invariant numerical fractions.

The first spectral channel grows and the second declines throughout the sampled segment for all five metrics, in both seeds. The third pair, near numerical floor in the Euclidean chart, increases to about `0.0183` with alpha=1 and `0.1055` with alpha=4. This shows additional metric-sensitive spectral structure; the top two remain separated.

## Controls

- Authenticated parent and four ancestors, with independent ZIP CRC/SHA-256 checks.
- Euclidean regression to v18.51b: maximum absolute discrepancy below `1e-13`.
- Positive-definite metrics, maximum frozen condition number 5.
- Algebraic source accounting within machine precision.
- Independent weighted-time-derivative check: maximum relative discrepancy approximately `5.55e-8` over the 790 rows.
- Deliberate non-orthogonal passive chart transformation with metric pullback: invariance residual near machine precision.

The verified machine-readable thresholds and environment live in `results/report.json`. Differences at the last digits across NumPy/SciPy versions are permitted only if source identity passes and numerical criteria are preserved.

## Interpretation boundary

This audit *does not* identify a physical kinetic metric, prove material covariance, or establish a physical cause. The split between generator drift, projector posture, and canonical connection is an algebraic attribution of a chosen skew-block observable. For a time-varying metric the additional whitening-rate term depends on this chosen convention. The genuinely kinetic/energetic geometry would need a derivation from the model's action or kinetic form and another independent audit.

The hypothesis of two moving edges or a narrow-band activator is not tested here. The two inputs are seed preparations at one midpoint kappa, **not two concurrent kappa boundaries**.

## Next calibration

Derive a metric from the Q550 Lagrangian/kinetic form, if available, then compare source attributions in a *metric-compatible* transport law, including the geometric connection generated by that kinetic metric. Until then this is a numerical robustness diagnostic within the declared model-Jacobian-Gram family.
