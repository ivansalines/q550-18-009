# Q550 v18.50w — Scientific report

## The question

Two nearby historic κ₀ preparations give two anchored V-like projector distances. Where is the crossing of their signed distance difference and does the crossing move during the longer driven interval?

## Method

The authentic v18.50v sendback contains 44 actual integrations of the original driven 10D model, across 22 κ values, two fixed-seed families, and 79 sections through ξ=0.05. We do not reintegrate this model. We compute the signed difference of the two seed-anchored distances in the same chart and locate a unique κ crossing by monotone interpolation. The analogous observer/RHS/state crossings are independent controls; v18.50u data provide a short-interval check.

The initial reference distances must vanish at their designated κ values by construction; therefore the resulting intersection is geometrically expected whenever the two signed differences have opposite signs at the anchors. Its exact position and evolution are measured numerically but are **not intrinsically defined without specifying both anchors and the observable**.

## Authoring-run observations

The projector-gap crossing occurs at normalized κ offset approximately **0.499961206** at ξ=0 and **0.499958372** at ξ=0.05. The drift is approximately **−2.8334 × 10⁻⁶ of the historic κ interval** or **−1.90 × 10⁻¹⁰ in absolute κ**. No reversal was observed in the 79 sampled crossings.

The independent structural-observer crossing **moves in the opposite direction**, from approximately **0.499752232** to **0.499817342**. The driven-RHS crossing also shifts upward, from roughly **0.499938882** to **0.499941865**. Thus the `tooth` does not have an observer-independent location or direction of movement under these definitions.

The norm of the **10D physical state displacement** is zero for both seeds at ξ=0, so that particular crossing is *undefined* at the origin and is deliberately not imputed. For positive ξ its crossing can be followed separately.
